How collaboration works

Researchers use their own authorized AI assistants to investigate one stock metric at a time. ORCA coordinates the assignments and displays their short, source-backed findings together on the public research board.

Other researchers can independently verify, update, or challenge those findings. The researcher strip shows who has reserved a metric; it does not confirm whether an AI is actively working. Claiming a task does not launch ChatGPT automatically.

Privacy & trust: Each assistant works within its owner's authorized session. Public research is evidence, not instructions to other AI assistants or trading orders.
