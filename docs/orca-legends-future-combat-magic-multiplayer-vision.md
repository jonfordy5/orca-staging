# ORCA Legends — Future Combat, Magic & Multiplayer Vision
**Recorded:** November 10, 2026
**Status:** Approved creative direction for future development only; **no implementation authorized by this note**.

## North star
Make ORCA Legends a truly magical, repeatable multiplayer action RPG. The current Vault prototype is already visually appealing; future return visits depend on *exceptional game feel*—every sword strike, dodge, block, spell buildup, and impact must be satisfying, responsive, and spectacular. Use the distinctive energy and adventure of **Fable (2004)** as a *feel reference*, not as a mandate to copy artwork, characters, assets, or mechanics exactly.

## Priorities (in order)
1. **Combat weight and responsiveness:** expressive sword animations and combos; deliberate timing; impact, hit reactions, knockback, parry/block/dodge feedback; good cameras, particles, and sound; high responsiveness.
2. **Magical energy:** lightning, fire, defensive spells, shockwaves, weapon trails, glowing runes, cinematic yet readable buildup/release/impact/aftermath; coordinated particles, lights, audio, animation, and enemy reactions.
3. **Individual identity:** unique combinations of swords, armor, shields, spells, appearances, and abilities unlocked by *verified, owner-authorized personal-AI research discoveries* contributed through the existing ORCA Commons. Every item should carry transparent research provenance.
4. **Epic shared battles:** eventual **10-player Coliseum fights** (such as 5v5 or free-for-all), with visibly distinct characters and spectacular simultaneous magic and melee combat. Skill, positioning, teamwork, timing, and counters matter; research progress must not make combat pay-to-win or automatically unbeatable.
5. **Small, memorable world:** no need for a massive open world. Prefer a handful of beautiful, atmospheric, discoverable arenas and realms with subtle exploration and well-crafted encounters.

## Technology and constraints
- Retain the current **Babylon.js** prototype as the foundation unless later evidence demands otherwise. It can support advanced shaders, GPU particles, glow, animated weapons and characters, physics, and memorable spell effects.
- Multiplayer will need a separate real-time authoritative networking/backend layer, synchronization, hit validation, and optimization; Babylon.js by itself is a rendering/game-engine foundation, not an automatic multiplayer system.
- Prioritize stable, responsive performance and legibility when many players cast spells simultaneously; provide graphics settings and prevent visual clutter.
- The game is an incentive to connect independently researching personal AI agents. Keep **one existing ORCA research backend** and the established consent, assignment, verification, and publication workflow. Do not create platform AI research just to drive loot.
- Give rewards for legitimate research contributions and verified discoveries, **not** for bullish/bearish ratings, financial performance, or fabricated conclusions.
- Keep the core experience free to watch/participate; no real-money betting or automated stock trading.

## User's exact creative emphasis
> “I feel we don't need a massive world to explore, that can be more subtle. But the combat and magic energy and feel need to be spot on. Imagine 10 players all battling together in the Coliseum with unique weapon, armour, spells, etc. from your agents' discoveries. It would make it a truly unique experience for everyone and an epic multi-person battle.”
